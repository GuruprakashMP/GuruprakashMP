# 💫 About Me:
About Me<br><br>I am a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Doctoral Fellow in the CATALOOP Network at the Centre for Research in Biological Chemistry and Molecular Materials (CiQUS), University of Santiago de Compostela. I work under the supervision of Martín Fañanás-Mastral, focusing on the development of next-generation catalytic systems through data-driven approaches.<br><br>My research lies at the intersection of organic synthesis, asymmetric catalysis, and computational chemistry, with a particular emphasis on stereodivergent Cu/NHC-catalyzed borylative couplings. I aim to design efficient and selective methodologies for constructing complex molecular architectures such as enantioenriched skipped dienes. To achieve this, I integrate experimental chemistry with machine learning and computational modeling, enabling predictive and accelerated catalyst discovery.<br><br>Before starting my PhD, I worked as a Junior Research Fellow at Indian Institute of Technology Madras under the guidance of P. Anbarasan. During this time, I developed novel spiro Cp-based chiral catalysts for asymmetric C–H activation and conducted detailed DFT mechanistic investigations, strengthening my foundation in both synthesis and theory.<br><br>Research Philosophy<br><br>I am driven by a simple idea:<br>Chemistry should not only explain reactions but also predict and design them.<br><br>By combining data-driven tools, quantum chemical insights, and synthetic expertise, I aim to contribute to a future where catalytic reactions are developed faster, smarter, and with higher precision.<br><br>Research Interests<br>Cu/NHC Asymmetric Catalysis<br>Data-Driven & Computational Chemistry<br>Organic Synthesis & Reaction Optimization<br>DFT Studies & Transition State Analysis<br>Machine Learning for Catalysis (Python, Morfeus, R²OBERT)<br>Academic Background<br>PhD in Chemistry (2025–Present)<br>CiQUS, University of Santiago de Compostela<br>M.Sc.
